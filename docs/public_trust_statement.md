# Public trust statement

Admissio helps universities and colleges plan how many admission offers to make so that they fill their places without overcrowding. To do this we forecast the share of admitted students likely to enrol at each institution.

**What we forecast.** Our forecasts describe institutions, not people. The model learns from published totals such as applications, offers and last year's enrolment. It never sees an applicant's name, scores, background or application, and it plays no part in deciding who is admitted.

**Who decides.** Forecasts advise admissions staff. Every offer plan is approved by a named member of staff at the institution, and anyone affected by an admissions decision keeps the right to have it reviewed by a person.

**How we check fairness.** We measure accuracy separately for smaller institutions, private for-profit institutions and minority-serving institutions, and we publish the results, including where the model falls short. Today the model is less accurate for small and for-profit institutions, so it remains a candidate and is not approved for release. Those institutions receive wider ranges so that no one mistakes an uncertain forecast for a precise one.

**How we stay accountable.** An Ethics Review Panel that includes each partner's data protection lead and an independent adviser approves every release. Every forecast is stored with the explanation that accompanied it so it can be audited. Problems are logged as incidents and resolved in the open, and the model will be retired if it cannot be made trustworthy.

**How to raise a concern.** Applicants and staff can raise a concern through the admissions office of the institution, which forwards it to Admissio and its Ethics Review Panel.
